# Kestrel Freight - Shipping, Insurance and Claims Policy

## 1. Service Levels and Zones

Kestrel Freight operates a regional parcel network covering six domestic zones. Zone A is the Kestrel metro area, Zone B covers the adjacent coastal ring, and Zones C through F cover the remaining inland corridors. Transit times are quoted in working days and exclude public holidays.

## 2. Transit Times

Standard service is offered in every zone and is quoted at 3 working days for Zone A and 5 working days for Zones B through F.

Express service is offered only in Zones A, B and C. Express transit is 1 working day in Zone A and 2 working days in Zones B and C.

Consignments for Zones D, E and F cannot be sent using Express service.

Saturday delivery is available in Zone A for an additional surcharge of 4.50 credits. No delivery is offered on Sundays or on public holidays.

Perishable consignments must be handed over for dispatch before 16:00 local time to qualify for next-working-day handling.

## 3. Pricing

The base charge is 3.20 credits for the first item and 1.40 credits for each additional item in the same consignment.

Oversized consignments, meaning any item whose longest side exceeds 90 centimetres, carry a surcharge of 6 credits.

Residential delivery carries a surcharge of 1.10 credits per consignment. Collection from a Kestrel service point is free.

## 4. Insurance Cover

Kestrel insures consignments against accidental loss and damage up to a declared value. The maximum declared value for a single consignment is 800 credits.

The sender must mark the consignment as fragile if the contents are sensitive to impact. Fragile-marked consignments that are damaged in transit are replaced rather than reimbursed at the declared value.

Consequential loss, including loss of profit and business interruption, is excluded from cover under all service levels.

## 5. Claims

Claims must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the delivery date. Claims submitted after that window are declined.

Claims are adjudicated by a Kestrel claims assessor within 5 working days of receipt. Where the evidence supplied is insufficient to establish the cause of damage, the claim may be referred for physical inspection, which adds a further 10 working days.

Approved claims are settled by credit note against the sender's account. Cash settlement is not available for commercial accounts.

## 6. Prohibited Items

Loose lithium cells, unframed blades and pressurised aerosols are not accepted. Firearms and ammunition require a registered firearms dealer account.

Perishable food items are accepted only when packed with dry ice and declared in advance.

Livestock, and any consignment containing live animals, is prohibited.

## 7. Accounts and Payment

Accounts are invoiced monthly in arrears and payment is due within 21 days of the invoice date.

Commercial accounts must hold a credit limit of at least 500 credits to open an account. The credit limit is reviewed every quarter.
