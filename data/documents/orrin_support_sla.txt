# Orrin Retail Group - Customer Support Service Levels

## 1. Support Channels

Orrin Retail Group sells household appliances under the Orrin brand and operates an in-house support centre.

Customer support is available by telephone, email and live chat. Live chat is staffed between 08:00 and 20:00 local time, every day except Sunday. Email support is monitored continuously but is not staffed on Sunday.

Telephone support is offered for a fee of 2.00 credits per call for non-warranty enquiries. Warranty-related telephone calls are free.

## 2. Response Times

Live chat is answered within 2 minutes during staffed hours. Email enquiries receive a first response within 1 working day.

Warranty claims receive a decision within 5 working days of receiving the claim. The decision is communicated by email.

## 3. Warranty

Appliances carry a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing faults. Accidental damage and consumable parts are not covered.

The warranty period starts on the delivery date recorded by the courier, not on the order date.

Extended warranty can be purchased within 60 days of delivery for a fee of 24 credits and adds 3 years of cover for parts and labour.

## 4. Returns and Refunds

Unused appliances in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The refund is issued to the original payment method within 14 days of the returned item being received and inspected.

Refurbished appliances are sold with a 12-month warranty and may not be returned except where the unit is faulty on delivery.

A 25 percent restocking fee applies to returns of opened appliances that are not faulty, provided the item is returned within 30 days.

## 5. Escalation

Unresolved enquiries are escalated to a senior adviser after 3 separate contacts about the same issue. Complaints about the conduct of a support agent are handled separately and are never counted towards the 3-contact escalation threshold.

Claims above 400 credits in value are referred to the Orrin dispute panel, which responds within 20 working days.

## 6. Feedback

Customers are asked to rate the support interaction out of 5. Ratings below 3 are reviewed by the support quality team within 10 working days.

Customer satisfaction scores are reported to the Orrin board each quarter.
